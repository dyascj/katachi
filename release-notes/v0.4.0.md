A lot of you are on Linux, so this release starts there: Katachi now installs like a real Linux app, runs on far more distributions, and gets Linux shortcuts right. Then it closes a long list of everyday gaps with Figma, from smart spacing and ruler guides to interactive components, angular gradients, grid auto layout, and Tailwind output.

## Linux

- **Packages.** Each release now has a `.deb`, an `.rpm`, and a tarball, for x86_64 and arm64. They install the app, its icons, an app-menu entry, and the `.katachi` file type, so double-clicking a file opens it.
- **Runs on older systems.** Builds now target glibc 2.28, so Katachi runs on Debian 11, Ubuntu 20.04, RHEL and Rocky 8 and 9, and everything newer. The 0.3 build needed glibc 2.35. Every package was installed and launched on Debian, Ubuntu, Rocky, Fedora, and openSUSE before release.
- **Tabs in the title bar.** Katachi draws its own title bar with the tabs in it and its own window buttons. Drag the strip to move, double-click it to maximize, and resize from any edge. If you'd rather have your desktop's title bar, turn on Settings > Appearance > "Use the system title bar".
- **No more black window.** The window used to be created transparent, which drew black without a compositor and on some drivers. It's opaque now unless glass was on when Katachi started.
- **Shortcuts that work.**
  - Ctrl G groups again; it was being taken by layout guides.
  - Ctrl Alt V pastes properties again; it was running distribute.
  - The commands Figma binds to the Control key on a Mac use their Windows and Linux keys: layout guides Ctrl Shift 4, use as mask Ctrl Alt M, and distribute and tidy up Alt Shift H / V / T. Ctrl Alt T stays free for your terminal.
  - Ctrl Q quits.
- **Menus speak Linux.** Every menu, tooltip, and the shortcuts list shows Ctrl, Alt, and Shift instead of Mac symbols.
- **Desktop integration.**
  - The System theme follows your desktop's light or dark setting.
  - Wayland and X11 match the window to its menu entry, so the dock shows the right icon.
  - "Show in folder" selects the file in your file manager.
  - The window title stays correct as you switch tabs.
  - Pasting images works on Wayland without XWayland.
- **Safer updates.** The updater picks the right build for your CPU and test-runs it before swapping it in, so it can never leave you with a build that won't start. It falls back to `wget` when `curl` isn't installed. Copies installed from a package leave updates to your package manager.

## Canvas

- Smart guides snap to equal spacing, with Figma's pink gap markers.
- Select evenly spaced layers and drag the pink handles between them to respace them all at once. Drag a layer's center knob onto another to swap their places.
- Ruler guides: with rulers on (Shift R), drag a guide out of a ruler. Guides are saved with the page, layers snap to them, and dragging one back onto the ruler deletes it.
- The Zoom tool (Z): click to zoom in, Alt-click to zoom out, or drag a box. Hold Z to use it for a moment.
- N and Shift N step through the frames on the page.
- The pixel grid (Shift ') and snap to pixel grid (Shift Cmd ') can be turned off.
- Right-click has Select layer (every layer under the pointer), Move to page, Add auto layout, and flips. Right-clicking empty canvas offers Lock all, Unlock all, and Hide UI.

## Layers and selection

- Shift-click selects a range of layers, and dragging moves every selected layer together.
- The layers list scrolls to whatever you select on the canvas.
- Undo and redo bring back the selection and the page the change was on.
- Select inverse (Shift Cmd A). Edit > Select all with same fill, stroke, effect, font, or instance. Cmd A skips locked layers.
- Batch rename: Cmd R with several layers selected renames them all with `$&` (current name), `$n` and `$N` (numbers), or match and replace, with a preview.
- Resize to fit (Alt Shift Cmd R), Remove fill, Lock all and Unlock all, and Paste over selection (Shift Cmd V).
- Paste a screenshot or any image from the clipboard. Drop an image on a layer that has an image fill, or hold Alt, to replace its image.
- Pages can be dragged to reorder and duplicated, and a page named `---` becomes a divider.

## Color and paint

- Angular and diamond gradients, and elliptical radial gradients.
- Gradient handles on the canvas while the gradient editor is open: drag the ends, the width handle, and the stops, or click the line to add a stop.
- The eyedropper: press I, or use the button in any color picker. A loupe shows the pixels under the pointer.
- The color picker takes Hex, RGB, CSS, HSL, or HSB, and lists the colors on the page and your color styles.
- Drag fills and strokes to reorder them, and give each its own blend mode.
- Shift X swaps fill and stroke. Number keys set opacity, as in Figma: 5 is 50%, and 4 then 5 is 45%.

## Effects

- Figma's newer effects: noise (mono, duo, or multitone), texture, and glass, with frost, refraction, depth, dispersion, and a light angle.
- Layer and background blurs can be progressive, ramping from one amount to another across the layer.
- Drop shadows have "Show behind transparent areas". It's off by default, as in Figma, so shadows no longer show through see-through fills unless you turn it on.
- Drag effects to reorder them. Background blur and glass now show up in exports.

## Layout and vectors

- Grid auto layout: rows, columns, gaps, fill or fixed tracks, and children that span cells.
- Text baseline alignment and canvas stacking order (first on top) for auto layout.
- Drag an auto layout frame's gap and padding handles right on the canvas. Arrow keys move a child through the flow instead of nudging it.
- Vector editing:
  - Marquee-select points.
  - Cmd-drag to bend.
  - Cut a path with Y.
  - Join two ends with Cmd J.
  - Continue an open path with the pen.
  - Polygons and stars can be edited.
- The pencil's smoothing is a setting.

## Text

- Figma's text shortcuts: Cmd B, I, and U, Shift Cmd X, Alt Cmd L / T / R / J to align, and steps for size, weight, letter spacing, and line height. They apply to the selected characters while you edit, or to whole layers.
- Real italic faces and every installed weight, listed the way Figma lists them.
- Missing fonts are flagged, and Replace fonts swaps them across the file in one step.
- Links: select text and press Cmd K. Links open in presentation mode and export to SVG.
- Justified text, paragraph indent, and vertical trim.

## Design systems

- Variables:
  - Values can be aliases of other variables.
  - Slash names group them.
  - Scopes ("Show in") decide which properties offer them.
  - Each one can have code syntax for web, iOS, and Android.
  - They bind to many more properties: typography, min and max sizes, and effects.
- Fills and strokes get Figma's "Apply variable" button.
- Components:
  - Descriptions.
  - Preferred values for instance swaps.
  - Exposed nested instances.
  - Push changes to main component.
  - Reset one property at a time.
- Styles open their editor straight from Local styles, folders can be renamed, and layout grids can be styles too.

## Libraries

Any `.katachi` file can be a library now, the way team libraries work in Figma.

- Open Libraries from the Assets tab and add files. Their components show in Assets, and their styles and variables show in the pickers under the library's name.
- Placing a library component copies it, and everything it needs, into your file, so the file still opens and renders without the library.
- When a library changes, a "Library updates available" notice lets you review the changes and update all or one at a time. Your overrides on instances are kept.
- Publish library in the library's Assets tab decides when other files see its changes.
- Libraries that were moved or renamed are found again. Library copies can be moved into your file to become your own.

## Prototyping

- Interactive components: Change to switches an instance to another variant while presenting.
- New triggers: mouse enter, mouse leave, on drag, and key or gamepad. New actions: scroll to, swap overlay, and set variable.
- Easing curves, custom bezier, and Figma's springs (Gentle, Quick, Bouncy, Slow).
- Overlays can be positioned, close when you click outside, and have their own background.
- Scrolling frames (horizontal, vertical, or both), with fixed and sticky layers.
- Device frames for presenting: iPhone, Pixel, iPad, MacBook, or Desktop.

## Dev Mode and export

- Generated code uses your variable and style names. Tailwind joins CSS, SwiftUI, and Compose.
- Mark frames Ready for dev, and pin annotations to layers.
- Hovering an auto layout frame in Dev Mode shows its padding and gaps.
- Export at any scale or size by typing 2.5x, 512w, or 512h. Export several layers at once, export to WebP, and set JPG quality.
- Shift Cmd E opens an Export dialog listing everything on the page that has export settings.
- Copy as code.

## Safer files

- A NaN or infinite number could make a file impossible to open. Those files open again, and new saves can't produce them.
- Files with a damaged layer tree are repaired when they open, and pasting malformed layers can't crash or hang.
- Saves are flushed to disk before they replace the file, symlinks and permissions are kept, unused images are left out, and saving without changes doesn't add a version.
- Every tab autosaves, not just the one on screen. Crash recovery no longer offers another open window's autosave.
- Updates never restart over unsaved work. Signatures are tied to their version.
- A script stuck in a loop is stopped after 10 seconds instead of freezing the app.
- Very large text, very large images, and very fine dash patterns no longer crash or freeze the canvas.

## Multiplayer and security

- Cursor chat: press / to type a message by your cursor that everyone in the session sees.
- Click someone's avatar to follow their view, or use Spotlight me to have everyone follow yours.
- Others' selections show their name, and you can see their text caret as they type.
- Comments take @mentions, filter by unresolved, resolved, yours, or mentions, and show an unread dot on threads with new replies.
- The session window shows the address to share with a Copy button, who's in the session, and whether you're connected. The host can remove people, and a dropped connection reconnects on its own without losing your edits.
- Sessions now have a code, shared as `host:port/code`. Connections without it are refused.
- When the host leaves, the session closes for everyone.
- Joining an address that doesn't answer gives up after 5 seconds.
- Collaborators' edits mark the file unsaved, and expanding layers is no longer shared.
- The MCP server refuses requests from web pages, so a site you visit can't drive the open document.
- `katachi serve` refuses to start on a file it can't read, instead of replacing it with an empty one.
